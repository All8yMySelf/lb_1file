    How To Play

    Drag or use WASD/Arrows to move your base.

    The cannon fires automatically at enemies within range.

    Destroy foes to earn credits and buy upgrades from the ring.

    Survive each wave and watch for bosses as the timer counts down.

    Click the info upgrade again or press play to resume.
