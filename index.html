    How To Play

    Enemies approach from beyond sensor range. Move the base with WASD, arrows, or drag, then keep threats inside weapon range long enough to destroy them.

    Controls Fire is automatic by default. Press Space for manual fire, F for auto-fire, M for Macross missiles, H for hotkeys, I for Enemy Stats, and O for ring info.
    Build Plan Buy Cannon, Laser, Missiles, Sensors, Defense, and Time Control upgrades. Hover over left-menu buttons for current stats and compact upgrade notes.
